Government Exam Admit Cards 2026

Track admit card release dates and download links

How to Download

1

Visit Official Site

Go to the official website of the conducting body.

2

Find Admit Card Section

Look for "Admit Card" or "Hall Ticket" link on the homepage.

3

Enter Credentials

Use your registration number, date of birth, or roll number to log in.

4

Download & Print

Save as PDF and print on A4 paper. Keep at least 2 copies.

5

Verify All Details

Check name, photo, exam centre, date, time, and reporting time.

Exam Day Checklist

  • Printed admit card (2 copies)
  • Valid photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License)
  • Passport-size photographs (2-3 extra)
  • Black/blue ballpoint pens
  • Transparent water bottle
  • Analog watch (smartwatches not allowed)

Items NOT Allowed

  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, or electronic devices
  • Calculators (unless specified by the exam body)
  • Bags, wallets, or purses inside the exam hall
  • Bluetooth devices, earphones, or headphones
  • Books, notes, or any printed/written material
  • Food items (except transparent water bottle)

Important: Verify These Details on Your Admit Card

Personal Details

Name, Father's name, Date of Birth, Category

Photo & Signature

Clear photograph and signature matching your registration

Exam Details

Exam name, date, shift/session, paper code

Centre Details

Centre name, address, city, and centre code

Timing

Reporting time, gate closure time, and exam start time

Roll Number

Your unique roll number / registration number
