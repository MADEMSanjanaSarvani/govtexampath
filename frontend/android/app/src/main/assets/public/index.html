Your Complete Guide to Government Exams in India

GovtExamPath is India's comprehensive career guidance platform built exclusively for government job aspirants. Every year, millions of candidates compete in over 500 competitive examinations conducted by central and state government bodies — from UPSC and SSC to IBPS and RRBs. Navigating eligibility requirements, application deadlines, syllabus patterns, and cut-off marks can be overwhelming. GovtExamPath simplifies this journey by bringing all the information you need into one unified, easy-to-use platform.

Our platform covers 16 major exam categories including UPSC Civil Services, SSC CGL and CHSL, Bank PO and Clerk examinations, Railway Group D and NTPC, Defence entries like NDA and CDS, State Public Service Commissions, Teaching exams such as CTET and UGC NET, Police recruitment, Insurance sector exams, PSU recruitment through GATE, and regulatory body examinations for RBI, SEBI, and NABARD.

What sets GovtExamPath apart is our suite of free tools. Our AI Career Guide helps you identify the best exams based on your educational background. The Eligibility Checker instantly tells you which exams you qualify for. Interactive Syllabus Mind Maps help you visualize and plan your preparation. The Exam Priority Matrix helps you decide which exams to focus on by comparing difficulty, vacancies, and salary.

All data on GovtExamPath is verified through official government sources and our automated verification system that checks for updates daily. Whether you are a first-time aspirant or an experienced candidate, GovtExamPath provides reliable, up-to-date information and preparation tools — completely free of charge.
