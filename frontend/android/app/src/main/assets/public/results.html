How to Check Your Result

  1. 1

    Visit the official website of the conducting body using the direct links provided above for each exam.

  2. 2

    Navigate to the "Results" or "Score Card" section on the official website.

  3. 3

    Enter your roll number, registration number, date of birth, or other required credentials to log in.

  4. 4

    View your result on screen. Download and save the scorecard or result PDF for your records.

  5. 5

    Check the cut-off marks and category-wise qualifying criteria. If selected, look for further instructions regarding document verification or the next stage of the recruitment process.

Results are declared on the official conducting body website. We provide direct links for your convenience. Always verify your result from the official source and keep a copy of your scorecard for future reference.
